Synthetic partner declaration, format 3 of 4: clause-numbered requirements.
Legal-adjacent register, "shall" and "must", numbered clauses, cross-references
between clauses. Precise about obligation, vague about payload.

Written to be a good requirements document and a poor interface contract. It
says what the consortium must do and never says what Meridian will send, in what
form, on what schedule, or to whom. Meridian is complied with rather than
coupled to, and this document is why.

Values are grounded in the worldbuilding research and are synthetic. Keep the
filename: parse.py dispatches on it.


MERIDIAN RISK LABORATORY
ARES resilience evidence requirements
Document MRL-ARES-ER-011, revision B, issued 2029-02-02


1.  SCOPE

1.1 This document states the evidence the Laboratory requires in order to
    issue a resilience opinion on a proposed ARES settlement configuration.

1.2 It applies to any configuration submitted for opinion. It does not
    constrain how a configuration is arrived at.

1.3 Compliance with this document is a precondition of an opinion. It is not
    a guarantee of a favourable one.


2.  ACCEPTABLE SERVICE LEVELS

2.1 The habitat atmosphere shall be maintained at a total pressure of not
    less than 34.5 kPa and not more than 103 kPa.

2.2 Diluent gas shall constitute not less than 30 percent of the atmosphere
    by partial pressure at all operating pressures.

2.3 Carbon dioxide partial pressure shall not exceed 3 mmHg on any rolling
    one-hour basis.

2.4 Oxygen partial pressure shall remain within the band appropriate to the
    total pressure selected under clause 2.1.

2.5 Potable water availability shall not fall below 2 sols of demand at the
    prevailing population.

2.6 An excursion beyond any limit in clauses 2.1 to 2.5 shall be reported
    with its duration, its depth and the population exposed.

2.7 The limits in this section are minima for an opinion. A configuration
    that meets them is not thereby judged resilient.


3.  STRESS CASES THE MODEL MUST BE ABLE TO REPRESENT

3.1 A regional dust event reducing incident insolation for a period of not
    less than 20 sols.

3.2 A planet-encircling dust event reducing incident insolation for a period
    of not less than 120 sols.

3.3 Loss of a single generation unit for the duration of its repair.

3.4 Loss of an oxygen generation train concurrent with clause 3.1.

3.5 Loss of a water recovery train concurrent with a resupply window slip of
    one synodic period.

3.6 A pressure loss event in one habitat module during any of clauses 3.1
    to 3.5.

3.7 The Laboratory shall specify the compound cases to be run. The list at
    clauses 3.4 to 3.6 is illustrative and is not exhaustive.


4.  REPRESENTATION REQUIREMENTS

4.1 The model shall represent the concurrence of clauses 3.1 to 3.6 rather
    than their individual occurrence.

4.2 Where two stressors share a physical cause, the model shall not treat
    them as independent.

4.3 The model shall carry a representation of uncertainty on any quantity
    used to support a resilience claim. A point estimate is not evidence.

4.4 The model shall be able to state, for any reported excursion, which
    subsystem failed and in what order.

4.5 Clause 4.2 is not satisfied by running each stressor separately and
    combining the results.


5.  EVIDENCE REQUIRED WITH A SUBMISSION

5.1 The configuration under opinion, as a complete set of installed
    capacities and operating limits.

5.2 The demand basis, including the population state assumed and the
    provenance of that assumption.

5.3 The results of every case required under clause 3, at the resolution at
    which the excursion limits in clause 2 are evaluated.

5.4 A statement of what the submission does not establish.

5.5 Sufficient information to reproduce the results in clause 5.3.


6.  GENERAL

6.1 The Laboratory does not model. It states what shall be demonstrated and
    judges what is submitted.

6.2 Failure to satisfy any clause of section 4 shall cause a submission to
    be returned without opinion.

6.3 Queries on this document to the Laboratory's evidence secretariat.
